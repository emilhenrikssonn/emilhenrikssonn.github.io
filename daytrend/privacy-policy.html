Daytrend

Privacy Policy · Last updated 20 November 2026

Daytrend collects no personal data. There are no analytics, no trackers, no advertising, and no third-party services. Your diary never reaches a server the developer controls, because no such server exists.

What Daytrend stores, and where

Everything you write — your notes, your daily ratings, and the categories you choose to track — is stored on your iPhone.

  • On your device. Entries are saved in the app's own local database. If you never sign in to iCloud, they never leave the phone.
  • In your personal iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, your entries sync through Apple's CloudKit into your own private iCloud database, so your devices stay in step. This storage belongs to your Apple Account. The developer cannot read it, query it, or recover it, and holds no key to it. It is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • One local preference. Your choice of light or dark appearance is kept on the device under the key appTheme. Nothing else is stored this way.

Daytrend works entirely offline. iCloud sync is a convenience, not a requirement.

What the developer receives

Nothing you write, and nothing that identifies you. The app contains no code that sends data anywhere except Apple's own iCloud and payment services. Specifically, Daytrend does not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK, does not read your advertising identifier, does not ask for your contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, or health data, and does not show advertising.

Subscriptions

Daytrend offers an optional paid subscription. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your name, card details, or billing address — only the anonymous, aggregated sales and subscription figures Apple publishes to every developer. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Tracking

Daytrend does not track you, and does not share data with other companies for advertising or measurement. It will never show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to ask for.

Deleting your data

  • Delete an individual entry inside the app.
  • Delete the app to remove everything held on that device.
  • To remove the synced copy, open iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, select Daytrend, and delete its data. Because the data lives in your own iCloud, this is entirely in your hands — no request to the developer is needed, and none can be acted on.

Children

Daytrend is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy
