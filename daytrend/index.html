Daytrend

Support & help

Daytrend is a one-minute daily diary. Write a line about your day, rate the few things you actually care about, and let the app show you the patterns you would never spot on your own.

Common questions

How does syncing work?

Daytrend syncs through your own iCloud account, automatically and in the background. There is no account to create and no backup button to press. Sign in to iCloud on each device and your entries appear on all of them.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app works exactly as well — it simply keeps everything on that one device.

My entries aren't appearing on my other device

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, and that iCloud Drive is switched on in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. Sync also needs a network connection, and can take a minute or two to settle after a change.

Can I fill in a day I missed?

That is your choice, and you make it during onboarding. Allow late entries lets you go back and complete or correct any past day. Leaving it off locks each day at midnight, which keeps the record honest — a rating written three days later is not really the same rating.

You can change your mind at any time in Settings.

How do I change what I track?

Open the Categories screen to add, rename, reorder, or archive anything you rate. Archiving keeps the history intact but stops asking you about it day to day.

You can also pick from a catalogue of ready-made trackables grouped by area — wellbeing, work, money, health, social — or write your own.

What do the statistics mean?

Best days ranks your days by an average of the categories you chose to count towards the overall score. Links looks for correlations between two things you track — whether your productivity really does follow your sleep, for instance. A link needs at least seven days where you rated both, otherwise the number would be noise rather than a finding.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by the app. Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or tap Manage subscription in Daytrend's Settings screen.

Changed device, or reinstalled? Tap Restore on the subscription screen to bring your purchase back.

What happens to my writing if I stop subscribing?

You keep it, and you can always read it. Daytrend never hides what you have already written — a lapsed subscription makes the app read-only rather than locking you out of your own diary.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app to remove everything on that device. To clear the synced copy, open iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, select Daytrend, and delete its data. Your diary lives in your iCloud, so this is entirely in your hands.
